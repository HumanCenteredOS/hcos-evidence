# HCOS™ Evidence Architecture

**Document type:** Architecture  
**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Purpose

The HCOS™ Evidence Architecture explains how evidence moves from source discovery to responsible use, interpretation, publication, correction, and long-term stewardship.

## Architectural Layers

1. **Source Layer** — research, guidance, historical records, cultural materials, organizational records, community knowledge, and internal HCOS™ documents.
2. **Verification Layer** — source existence, metadata, provenance, version, relevance, quality, conflicts, and limitations.
3. **Classification Layer** — empirical evidence, authoritative guidance, historical evidence, cultural evidence, organizational evidence, HCOS™ framework evidence, interpretation, synthesis, and hypothesis.
4. **Interpretation Layer** — meaning, context, applicability, uncertainty, population, setting, system relationships, and human consequences.
5. **Integration Layer** — connections to HCOS™ foundations, disciplines, standards, methods, architectures, instruments, education, and case analysis.
6. **Governance Layer** — evidence boundaries, source transparency, citation integrity, human review, version history, correction, and supersession.
7. **Stewardship Layer** — monitoring, reassessment, updating, correction, reinterpretation, supersession, and retirement.

> Evidence must remain connected to its origin, context, limitations, interpretation, and intended use.
