# HCOS™ Evidence Hierarchy

**Document type:** Architecture  
**Status:** Foundational Draft

## General Priority Order

1. Original primary research or firsthand documented source
2. Official or authoritative source
3. Systematic review, meta-analysis, or evidence-based guideline
4. Strong peer-reviewed review article
5. Reputable government, academic, professional, or international organization
6. High-quality historical scholarship or journalism
7. Internal HCOS™ sources for proprietary definitions and framework history
8. Secondary commentary used cautiously

## Qualification

The appropriate hierarchy depends on the question. Clinical, legal, historical, cultural, organizational, and HCOS™ framework claims require different source types.

## Prohibited Substitution

Internal HCOS™ documents must not be used as proof that an external scientific, legal, clinical, or historical claim is true.
