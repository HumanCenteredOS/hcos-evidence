# HCOS™ Evidence Maturity Model

**Document type:** Architecture  
**Status:** Concept Draft

## Levels

### Level 1 — Unstructured
Sources are collected inconsistently; evidence and opinion may be blended.

### Level 2 — Documented
Sources, citations, and basic limitations are recorded.

### Level 3 — Governed
Standards, claim classification, citation audits, and human review are applied.

### Level 4 — Integrated
Evidence is linked across repositories, with provenance, synthesis records, and implementation use.

### Level 5 — Learning and Stewarding
Evidence is continuously monitored, corrected, reinterpreted, and updated with stakeholder knowledge.

This is an original HCOS™ developmental proposal and has not been independently validated.
