# HCOS™ Evidence Atlas

**Document type:** Architecture and Index  
**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Purpose

The HCOS™ Evidence Atlas maps external disciplines, theories, research traditions, and authoritative guidance to specific HCOS™ concepts.

## Atlas Record Structure

- Evidence domain
- Foundational sources
- Key theories or frameworks
- Relevant HCOS™ components
- Nature of support
- Direct or indirect relevance
- Evidence limitations
- HCOS™ extension
- Original HCOS™ contribution
- Research gaps

## Initial Domains

- Human Factors and Ergonomics
- Systems Engineering
- Implementation Science
- Learning Health Systems
- Patient Safety
- Organizational Science
- AI Governance
- Clinical Wisdom
- Compassion Science
- Attention and Situation Awareness
- Meaning and Work
- Community and Cultural Evidence

The atlas documents relationships. It does not imply that an external source validates an entire HCOS™ framework merely because it supports one component.
