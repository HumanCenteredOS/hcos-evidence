# HCOS™ Hustle & Flow™ Evidence Prompt

```text
Apply HCOS-NES-001 to the selected person, song, cultural story, or historical episode.

Organize the analysis as:
1. What Is Documented
2. What the Person Expressed
3. What Context Helps Explain
4. What Research Helps Explain
5. What the Evidence Cannot Tell Us
6. HCOS™ Human-Centered Systems Interpretation

Do not diagnose the person, treat every lyric as autobiography, infer private motivation as fact, erase agency, excuse harmful actions, or present allegations as established fact.
```
