# HCOS™ Systems Evidence Synthesis Prompt

```text
Synthesize the supplied evidence through an HCOS™ systems lens.

Identify the system, participants, pressures, protections, assumptions, incentives, constraints, information flows, authority, burden transfer, accountability, and redesign opportunities.

Separate documented findings, authoritative guidance, author interpretation, HCOS™ synthesis, proposed application, and uncertainty.

The system is the primary unit of analysis, but people retain responsibility within the authority, knowledge, and choices available to them.
```
