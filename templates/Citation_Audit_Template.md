# Citation Audit

| Claim or section | Citation | Source type | Direct or indirect support | Qualification added | Limitation | Verification status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Audit Checks

- [ ] Every citation supports the adjacent claim
- [ ] No citation exceeds its population, setting, or design
- [ ] No association is presented as causation
- [ ] Internal HCOS™ sources are not used as external validation
- [ ] Direct quotations were verified
- [ ] APA 7 references match in-text citations
- [ ] URLs and DOIs were checked when possible
- [ ] Superseded, corrected, or retracted sources were flagged
