# HCOS™ Literature Review Method

**Document type:** Method  
**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Minimum Process

1. Define the review question.
2. Define population, context, period, and intended use.
3. Select appropriate databases and authoritative sources.
4. Record search terms and date searched.
5. Establish inclusion and exclusion criteria.
6. Screen results for relevance.
7. Appraise quality and applicability.
8. Extract findings and limitations.
9. Identify evidence gaps.
10. Update the search when time-sensitive evidence may have changed.

A general PubMed search is evidence discovery, not a systematic review, unless systematic methods are fully specified and followed.
