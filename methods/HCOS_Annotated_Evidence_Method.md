# HCOS™ Annotated Evidence Method

**Document type:** Method  
**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Required Annotation Fields

- Evidence record ID
- Full APA 7 citation
- Source type and design
- Population and setting
- Primary question
- Key findings
- Relevant quotations or page locations
- Limitations
- Conflicts of interest
- HCOS™ concepts informed
- Direct or indirect support
- Potential misuse or overstatement risk
- Related evidence records
- Review date and reviewer

Annotations must not imply that a source supports HCOS™ concepts the source did not study.
