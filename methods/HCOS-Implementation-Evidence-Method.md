# HCOS™ Implementation Evidence Method

**Document type:** Method  
**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Before Implementation

Define the problem, review evidence, assess local context, identify barriers and facilitators, establish baseline conditions, and define intended outcomes and protections.

## During Implementation

Monitor fidelity, record adaptations, capture stakeholder experience, track burden transfer, identify emerging risks, and document decisions and assumptions.

## After Implementation

Evaluate outcomes, compare baseline and post-implementation conditions, examine unintended consequences, assess sustainability, determine what should be retained or revised, and update organizational knowledge.

Implementation evidence must include both operational outcomes and effects on the human system.
