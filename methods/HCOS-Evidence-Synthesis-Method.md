# HCOS™ Evidence Synthesis Method

**Document type:** Method  
**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Method

1. Define the systems question.
2. Identify relevant evidence types.
3. Appraise source quality and relevance.
4. Extract claims, context, limitations, and uncertainty.
5. Separate direct findings from interpretation.
6. Map evidence to specific HCOS™ concepts.
7. Identify interactions across system conditions.
8. Examine pressure, protection, burden transfer, agency, and accountability.
9. Develop a bounded HCOS™ synthesis.
10. Record evidence gaps and future research needs.

## Required Distinctions

- What the source found
- What the source authors concluded
- What HCOS™ infers
- What HCOS™ proposes
- What remains uncertain
