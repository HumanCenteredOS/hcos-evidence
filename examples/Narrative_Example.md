# Narrative Evidence Example

## What Is Documented
State confirmed biographical, historical, and contextual facts.

## What the Person Expressed
Describe documented interviews, public statements, or artistic expression.

## What Context Helps Explain
Describe relevant cultural, economic, community, legal, or industry conditions.

## What Research Helps Explain
Introduce research on comparable system patterns without claiming that it explains the whole person.

## What the Evidence Cannot Tell Us
Identify disputed facts, private motivations, missing records, and interpretive limitations.

## HCOS™ Interpretation
Explain possible lessons concerning pressure, protection, suffering, agency, leadership, and system design.
