# Community Evidence Example

## Question
How did a policy change alter pressure across an interconnected community system?

## Evidence
Public records, community testimony, local economic data, transportation and access data, historical context, and policy research.

## Systems Interpretation
Map who benefited, who carried additional burden, what protections existed, what protections were missing, where responsibility shifted, and what unintended consequences emerged.

Community-generated knowledge should not automatically be subordinated to outside institutional analysis.
