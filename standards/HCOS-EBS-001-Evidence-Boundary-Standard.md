# HCOS-EBS-001 — Evidence Boundary Standard

**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Required Labels

- Established by the cited source
- Supported by the cited source
- Contextually supported
- Author interpretation
- HCOS™ synthesis
- Proposed application
- Hypothesis
- Requires validation
- Not directly studied
- Disputed
- Uncertain
- Full text unavailable

## Boundary Rules

Do not treat correlation as causation, generalize beyond the evidence, diagnose from narrative evidence, infer private motives as fact, present allegations as proven, use internal HCOS™ documents as external validation, or claim validation without evidence for the exact instrument and use.

> The system may be the primary unit of analysis while people remain responsible within the authority, knowledge, and choices available to them.
