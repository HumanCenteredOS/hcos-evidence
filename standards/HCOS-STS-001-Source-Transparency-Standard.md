# HCOS-STS-001 — Source Transparency Standard

**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Minimum Source Record

- Author or responsible organization
- Title
- Publication or record date
- Source type
- Version or edition
- DOI or official URL when available
- Access limitations
- Retraction, correction, or supersession status
- Relevance to the claim
- Direct or indirect support

## Transparency Requirements

Cite disputed claims, identify inaccessible full text, name exact editions, separate background reading from cited sources, disclose contextual-only use, avoid hidden reliance on AI summaries, and document internal HCOS™ sources by identifier and version.

> A source should not merely be present. Its role in the reasoning should be visible.
