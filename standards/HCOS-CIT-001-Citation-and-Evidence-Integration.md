# HCOS-CIT-001 — Citation and Evidence Integration Standard

**Status:** Foundational Draft

## Requirements

Every evidence integration must:

- Identify the exact claim requiring support
- Match the claim to the strongest relevant source
- Verify that the source supports the wording
- Preserve distinctions between evidence, interpretation, synthesis, and hypothesis
- Use APA 7 unless another style is required
- Avoid invented metadata, quotations, findings, page numbers, URLs, or DOIs
- Identify indirect, preliminary, inaccessible, outdated, corrected, or superseded evidence
- Maintain identifiable human accountability

## Required Outputs

1. Revised document
2. Citation audit table
3. APA 7 bibliography
4. Evidence integration notes
5. Repository update record

Citation integration may make a document evidence-informed. It does not make an HCOS™ model, instrument, or framework validated.
